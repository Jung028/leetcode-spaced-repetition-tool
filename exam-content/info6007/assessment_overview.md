## Assessment Detail

- **Early Semester Feedback Task (5%)** — multiple choice / true-false, open book on Canvas, syllabus Weeks 1–3, held Week 4.
- **Weekly progress evaluation (10%)** — conducted during tutorials, based on the progressive project board (GitHub), evaluates group project progress, held Weeks 6–12.
- **Group Assignment (25%)** — groups of 4–5 students formed within the same tutorial; submission in Week 13.
- **Final Exam (60%)** — restricted open-book exam, one A4 sheet allowed (double-sided, handwritten or typed), case-study based questions, held in the formal exam period.

## Hurdle Requirement

Students must achieve at least 40% in the written examination **and** obtain an overall final mark of 50 or more to pass the unit. Any student who does not meet these hurdle requirements may be awarded a maximum final mark of 45, regardless of their overall average.

## Best Project Certificates

Awarded to groups demonstrating outstanding project quality, strong application of concepts, and effective teamwork, to recognise engagement, collaboration, and excellence in learning.
