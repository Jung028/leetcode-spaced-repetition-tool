# INFO6007 — Project Management in IT — Unit Outline

Source: Week 01 lecture slides ("Introduction"), INFO6007 Project Management in IT, Semester 2 2026.

## Learning Outcomes

At the completion of this unit, you should be able to:

- **LO1**. Understand each of the core project management knowledge areas.
- **LO2**. Apply project management knowledge, tools and techniques.
- **LO3**. Demonstrate developed and refined project management skills.
- **LO4**. Develop basic proficiency in project management tools and practices.
- **LO5**. Demonstrate team and management skills.

## Schedule

- **Lecture**: every Thursday 7pm–9pm. Delivered face to face, with a live Zoom option. Recordings available on Canvas within 24 hours.
- **Tutorial**: enrolment required. Held Tuesday–Friday from Week 2, face to face. Attendance is important — weekly progress evaluations run Weeks 6–12. Attend the tutorial listed in your timetable.

## Expectations

- Attend scheduled classes and tutorials.
- Devote about 6–9 hours per week to study, apart from lectures and tutorials.
- Read weekly readings before attending lecture.
- Participate constructively — respect for one another, be humble.
- Check Canvas at least once a week; check the Ed Forum at least once a day.
- Notify academics of difficulties promptly; notify team members honestly and promptly about difficulties.
