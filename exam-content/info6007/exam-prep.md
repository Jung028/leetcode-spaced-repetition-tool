Exam format: 4 questions, with each question having a brief scenario and 3-4 sub-questions.

Duration: 2 hrs + 10 mins reading time

Mark distribution: Each question will carry 15 points

Scope: Exam can cover any topic from the lectures or tutorials. While the content from the reading material and guest lectures will not be tested, you could use them to strengthen your answers.

How to answer: Use the space provided under each sub-question in the question paper itself. If you run out of space, there would be extra pages at the end (make sure to indicate you have used this space inside the question. Clearly number your question in this space).

What can you bring: A two-sided cheat sheet (handwritten or typed), non-programmable calculator, Bilingual dictionary.

We will have exam-specific activities in tutorials 12 and 13 where you will be able to discuss questions with peers and answer model questions in the class. I am planning to revise the content in week 13 lecture and will release a practice paper in week 11. I also encourage you to try out all tutorial questions and questions at the end of the lecture slides for practice. You can also attend the fortnightly consultation sessions via zoom on every other Thursday at 10 AM (you can find the link on 'Zoom' tab on Canvas.

Let us know if you have any further concerns.
