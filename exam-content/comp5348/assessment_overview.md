## Overview

Assessments in this unit have been designed to foster deep learning and building graduate qualities. You can see all assessment tasks by clicking on the Assignments left side navigation item.

You are responsible for understanding University policies regarding unit assessment. Please consult Assessment Procedures in the Policy Register.

For all assessment tasks you can request an extension, this may be in the form of a Simple Extension or Special Consideration.

If you require ongoing support throughout the semester due to disability, we recommend registering with Disability Services who can organise any necessary adjustments for assessments for all your units throughout the semester. This approach negates the need to apply for Special Consideration for multiple assessment should your circumstances affect your ability to complete the tasks by the due date.

## Final Exam Format (announced by the lecturer, Week 8 lecture)

- **Question types:** mostly **multiple-choice** questions; **some fill-in-the-blank** questions; and **some short-answer scenario questions** — given a scenario, the student is asked to **design a specific architecture**.

- **Coverage:** all lectures, labs/tutorials and assessments (see the schedule above).

- **Assessment notes from the same lecture:** Assignment 1 is closed (similarity report is visible on Canvas after a PDF submission; a handwritten scan may not get one). No simple extensions in this unit — apply for Special Consideration if something urgent happens.

### How the question bank should follow this format

- Mirror the three exam styles: **multiple-choice** (`mcq` / multiple-answer), **fill-in-the-blank** (`fillblank` — one word or number per blank, e.g. a formula term or a computed value), and **scenario-style** questions ("given this system, which design / which resource / how many clients…"). While Phase 1 is active, scenario questions are written as multiple-choice or multiple-answer *scenario* questions (no free-text answers yet); typed short-answer architecture-design questions belong to Phase 2.
- Keep difficulty at "solid understanding of the calculations and the reasoning", not trick questions — the exam is aimed at a pass-the-hurdle level.
- Calculation-heavy weeks (e.g. Week 8 operational laws) should use **numeric worked-example** questions in all three styles.
