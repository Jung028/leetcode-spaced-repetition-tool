# COMP4348 / COMP5348 (NE) — Assessment Overview

Source: [Canvas — COMP4348 COMP5348 (NE)](https://canvas.sydney.edu.au/courses/73762)

## Overview

Assessments in this unit have been designed to foster deep learning and building graduate qualities. You can see all assessment tasks by clicking on the Assignments left side navigation item.

You are responsible for understanding University policies regarding unit assessment. Please consult Assessment Procedures in the Policy Register.

For all assessment tasks you can request an extension, this may be in the form of a Simple Extension or Special Consideration.

If you require ongoing support throughout the semester due to disability, we recommend registering with Disability Services who can organise any necessary adjustments for assessments for all your units throughout the semester. This approach negates the need to apply for Special Consideration for multiple assessment should your circumstances affect your ability to complete the tasks by the due date.

## Assessment Schedule

| Task | Description | Due Week | Weighting |
|------|-------------|----------|-----------|
| Assignment 1 | Paper-based exercises based on material of weeks 1-6. | Week 7 | 10% |
| Assignment 2 | Paper-based tasks on week 7-10 materials, & reflecting on lab experiences. | Week 11 | 10% |
| Group Project | Practical work integrating subsystems using communication technologies. | Week 12 | 10% |
| Group Presentation | Group presentation based on the Group Project. | Week 13 | 10% |
| Final Exam | Written questions covering all lectures, labs, and assessments. | Formal Exam Period | 60% |

It is a policy of the School of Computer Science that in order to pass this unit, a student must achieve at least 40% in the final examination. A student must also achieve an overall final mark of 50 or more.

Any student not meeting these requirements may be given a maximum final mark of no more than 45 regardless of their average.
